Echo Lab 3D - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Echo Lab 3D is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Echo Lab 3D YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page to publish their own videos and reply to comments on their own content.

What data the app uses

  • Access to the owner's own YouTube channel (upload videos, set thumbnails, read statistics, read and reply to comments).
  • Access to the owner's own Instagram account and Facebook Page (publish Reels, read statistics, read and reply to comments).
  • Public comments on the owner's own videos, only to write replies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
  • We do not access any other person's account.
  • We do not use data for advertising.

Storage

Access keys are stored encrypted (GitHub Actions secrets) and are only used by the owner's automated jobs. Channel statistics are kept to plan future videos.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Facebook's settings.
